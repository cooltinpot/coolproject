THE PARRY

  • Every enemy attack lands on the beat of the soundtrack. A shrinking gold ring marks the moment of impact.
  • Tap PARRY just before impact. The window is about 0.18 s. A perfect parry slows time, sends projectiles back, and damages the attacker's posture.
  • Press within 95 ms of impact, which lands right on the beat, for a PERFECT: a chord-tuned chime, double Resonance and more style. Every parry shows how early you pressed.
  • Enough parries break posture. Hit ATTACK on a broken enemy to RIPOSTE, an instant execution.
  • Red attacks can't be parried. Jump them or dash through them.
  • The Threat Lane at the bottom shows incoming hits sliding toward the center in time with the music.
  • Mashing doesn't work: a parry that misses locks you out for a moment. A successful parry resets the lockout, so you can chain them.
